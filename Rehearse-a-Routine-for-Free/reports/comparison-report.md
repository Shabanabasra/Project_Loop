## Why status alone is insufficient

Both runs above show the same Platform Status. If you only looked
at a status color or a single word, you could not tell these two
runs apart. The difference only appears once you actually open and
read the transcript: one run's transcript shows a real file being
read and a real summary being produced, and the other shows an
attempt to read a file that does not exist, caught safely, with no
summary ever produced.

## The Project 9 lesson (A5)

Green status alone cannot prove that the task itself succeeded.
The transcript must be read. A green status means the session ended
without an infrastructure error - nothing more. It does not mean the
task you actually asked for was completed.
